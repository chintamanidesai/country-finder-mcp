# Singapore Visa Rules for Indian Nationals

Country: Singapore

Applicable To: Indian Passport Holders

Visa Required: YES

Visa On Arrival: NO

eVisa Available: No direct self-service eVisa application

Visa Types:
- Tourist Visa
- Business Visa
- Multiple Entry Visa
- Transit Facility (subject to eligibility)

Visa Cost:

Government Visa Fee:
- SGD 30

Additional Charges:
- Authorized visa agent service fees may apply

Typical Processing Time:
- 3 to 5 working days

Maximum Stay:
- Usually up to 30 days per visit
- Subject to immigration approval

Application Process:

1. Prepare required documents
2. Submit application through authorized visa agent
3. Pay visa fee
4. Wait for processing
5. Receive electronic visa approval
6. Travel to Singapore

Required Documents:

Mandatory:
- Passport valid for at least 6 months
- Completed visa application
- Passport-size photograph
- Flight itinerary
- Accommodation details

Supporting Documents:
- Bank statements
- Employment proof
- Cover letter
- Additional documents if requested

Singapore Arrival Card (SG Arrival Card):

Required:
- YES

When To Submit:
- Within 3 days before arrival

Information Required:
- Passport details
- Travel details
- Accommodation information

Documents Immigration May Request:

- Passport
- Valid visa
- SG Arrival Card confirmation
- Return ticket
- Hotel booking
- Proof of funds

Entry Requirements:

Traveller should carry:
- Passport
- Visa approval
- Return or onward ticket
- Accommodation proof
- SG Arrival Card acknowledgement

Entry Restrictions:

- Tourist visa does not permit employment.
- Immigration officers may request additional supporting documents.
- Entry is not guaranteed solely because a visa has been issued.

Important Notes:

- Visa applications are generally submitted through authorized agents.
- SG Arrival Card is mandatory and separate from the visa.
- Passport should be valid for at least 6 months.
- Immigration authorities have final authority regarding admission.
- Multiple-entry visas are commonly issued but validity varies by applicant profile.
