# Malaysia Entry Rules for Indian Nationals

Country: Malaysia

Applicable To: Indian Passport Holders

Visa Required:

Current Situation:
- Indian nationals may be eligible for visa-free entry for short tourism visits under Malaysia's temporary visa exemption program.
- If visa-free eligibility does not apply, travellers must obtain an eVisa before travel.

Visa On Arrival: NO

eVisa Available: YES

Visa Types:
- Visa-Free Tourist Entry (when eligible)
- eVisa
- Business Visa
- Multiple Entry Visa

Visa Cost (for eVisa Applicants):

Government Charges:
- Visa Fee: Depends on nationality and visa category
- Processing Fee: RM 105
- Additional payment service charges may apply

Typical Processing Time:
- 2 to 5 working days

Maximum Stay:
- Visa-Free Entry: Up to 30 days (subject to current government policy)
- eVisa: Usually up to 30 days

Application Process (eVisa):

1. Complete online application
2. Upload required documents
3. Pay fees online
4. Receive approved eVisa electronically
5. Carry digital or printed eVisa
6. Present documents at immigration

Required Documents:

Mandatory:
- Passport valid for at least 6 months
- Passport-size photograph
- Return or onward flight ticket
- Accommodation confirmation

Financial Documents:
- Bank statements
- Proof of sufficient funds

Additional Documents:
- Travel itinerary
- Invitation letter (if applicable)
- Business documents (for business travel)

Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC):

Required:
- YES

When to Submit:
- Within 3 days before arrival

Information Required:
- Passport details
- Travel details
- Accommodation information

Documents Immigration May Request:

- Passport
- Approved eVisa (if applicable)
- MDAC confirmation
- Return ticket
- Hotel booking
- Proof of funds
- Supporting travel documents

Entry Restrictions:

- Employment is not permitted under tourist entry permission.
- Immigration officers may deny entry if supporting documents are insufficient.
- Traveller must satisfy immigration requirements at the port of entry.

Important Notes:

- Carry printed or digital copies of all travel documents.
- Passport should have at least 6 months validity.
- Ensure MDAC is completed before travel.
- Entry permission is always at the discretion of Malaysian immigration authorities.
- Visa and immigration rules may change without notice.

Arrival Checklist:

✓ Passport
✓ MDAC Submission
✓ Return Ticket
✓ Hotel Booking
✓ Proof of Funds
✓ eVisa (if required)
✓ Travel Insurance (recommended)
