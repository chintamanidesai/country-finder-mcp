# Vietnam Visa Rules for Indian Nationals

Country: Vietnam

Applicable To: Indian Passport Holders

Visa Required: YES

Visa On Arrival: NO (unless approved under special immigration arrangements)

eVisa Available: YES

Visa Types:
- Tourist eVisa
- Business eVisa
- Multiple Entry eVisa

Visa Cost:

Government Fees:
- Single Entry eVisa: USD 25
- Multiple Entry eVisa: USD 50

Typical Processing Time:
- 3 to 5 working days

Maximum Stay:
- Up to 90 days
- Single or Multiple Entry depending on visa issued

Application Process:

1. Complete online eVisa application
2. Upload passport information page
3. Upload passport-size photograph
4. Pay visa fee online
5. Wait for approval
6. Download approved eVisa
7. Present eVisa upon arrival

Required Documents:

Mandatory:
- Passport valid for at least 6 months
- Digital passport photograph
- Passport information page copy

Supporting Documents:
- Flight booking
- Hotel reservation
- Travel itinerary

Documents Immigration May Request:
- Return ticket
- Accommodation details
- Proof of sufficient funds

Entry Requirements:

Traveller should carry:
- Printed eVisa
- Passport
- Return or onward ticket
- Hotel booking confirmation

Entry Restrictions:

- Tourist visa does not permit employment.
- Immigration officers may request additional documentation.
- Entry may be denied if travel purpose cannot be verified.

Important Notes:

- Vietnam eVisa is usually the preferred option for Indian tourists.
- Verify that the intended port of entry supports eVisa entry.
- Passport should have at least 6 months validity.
- Immigration authorities have final discretion regarding admission.
