# Japan Visa Rules for Indian Nationals

Country: Japan

Applicable To: Indian Passport Holders

Visa Required: YES

Visa On Arrival: NO

eVisa Available: YES

Visa Types:
- Tourist eVisa
- Temporary Visitor Visa
- Business Visa
- Multiple Entry Visa
- Transit Visa

Visa Cost:
- Single Entry Visa: INR 500
- Multiple Entry Visa: INR 500
- Transit Visa: INR 50
- Additional VFS service charges apply

Typical Processing Time:
- 5 to 10 working days
- Apply at least 3 to 4 weeks before travel

Maximum Stay:
- Usually up to 90 days per visit
- Subject to visa issued

Application Process:

1. Prepare required documents
2. Complete visa application
3. Submit application through VFS
4. Pay applicable fees
5. Wait for embassy review
6. Receive eVisa or visa approval
7. Present visa and passport upon arrival

Required Documents:

Mandatory:
- Valid passport
- Visa application form
- Recent passport-size photograph
- Travel itinerary
- Flight reservation
- Hotel booking or accommodation proof

Financial Documents:
- Bank statements
- Income Tax Returns (recommended)
- Salary slips (if employed)

Employment Documents:
- Employment letter
- Leave approval letter
- Business registration documents (if self-employed)

Supporting Documents:
- Cover letter explaining trip purpose
- Additional documents requested by embassy

Entry Requirements:

Traveller may be asked to present:
- Passport
- Valid visa/eVisa
- Return or onward ticket
- Accommodation proof
- Proof of sufficient funds

Entry Restrictions:

- Paid employment is not allowed on a tourist visa.
- Final admission is determined by Japanese immigration authorities.
- Immigration officers may request additional supporting documents.

Multiple Entry Visa:

May be available for eligible travellers with:
- Strong travel history
- Previous visits to Japan
- Frequent business travel
- Strong financial standing

Important Notes:

- eVisa is available for eligible Indian applicants.
- Applications may be submitted up to 3 months before travel.
- Visa approval is never guaranteed.
- Do not make non-refundable travel commitments until visa approval is received.
- Passport should ideally have at least 6 months validity beyond travel dates.

Official Guidance:

- Apply through authorized VFS centres.
- Follow instructions published by the Embassy of Japan in India.
