# Indonesia Visa Rules for Indian Nationals

Country: Indonesia

Applicable To: Indian Passport Holders

Visa Required: YES

Visa On Arrival: YES

eVisa Available: YES

Visa Types:
- Visa On Arrival (VOA)
- Electronic Visa On Arrival (e-VOA)
- Tourist Visa
- Business Visa

Visa Cost:

Visa On Arrival:
- Approximately IDR 500,000

e-VOA:
- Approximately IDR 500,000

Typical Processing Time:

e-VOA:
- Usually 1 to 5 working days

Visa On Arrival:
- Issued upon arrival

Maximum Stay:

Initial Stay:
- Up to 30 days

Extension:
- May be extended once for an additional 30 days subject to immigration approval

Application Process (e-VOA):

1. Complete online application
2. Upload passport details
3. Pay visa fee
4. Receive e-VOA approval
5. Present passport and e-VOA at immigration

Application Process (VOA):

1. Arrive at eligible Indonesian airport or seaport
2. Pay VOA fee
3. Receive VOA
4. Proceed through immigration

Required Documents:

Mandatory:
- Passport valid for at least 6 months
- Return or onward ticket

Recommended:
- Hotel booking
- Travel itinerary
- Travel insurance

Documents Immigration May Request:
- Proof of accommodation
- Proof of sufficient funds
- Return travel confirmation

Entry Requirements:

Traveller should carry:
- Passport
- Return ticket
- Accommodation details
- e-VOA approval (if obtained online)

Entry Restrictions:

- Tourist visa does not allow employment.
- Overstaying visa validity may result in fines and immigration penalties.
- Entry decisions remain subject to immigration clearance.

Important Notes:

- e-VOA is generally faster than obtaining VOA at busy airports.
- Bali, Jakarta and major international airports support VOA/e-VOA entry.
- Passport must have at least one blank page.
- Immigration rules may change without notice.
